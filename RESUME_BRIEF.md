# Resume brief

## Goal

Start conversations with recruiters and get screening calls.

## Target

Infrastructure companies working on AI infrastructure, sandboxes, workflow execution, durable workflows, and orchestration. Open to companies in related areas.

## Layout constraint

Keep the final resume to one A4 page. New content must be prioritized, condensed, or rewritten to fit this limit; do not allow overflow onto a second page.

## Positioning

Engineer with a few years of experience, emphasizing recent infrastructure work. Exact years, roles, dates, and technologies remain to be gathered.

Recent project: Orchex, involving durable workflows. Ownership, implementation details, maturity, users, and results remain to be gathered; do not assume these facts.

## Working approach

User-provided old resume content is preserved in `CAREER_SOURCE.md`. Its profile, work, project, design, schema, and demo links are indexed in `RESUME_LINKS.md`, including LeetCode and commented-out projects. Consult these records when gathering experience; do not treat saved claims as independently verified.

Prioritize what to include and how to explain it before formatting. Use the locally installed dabydat resume-builder skill at `.agents/skills/resume-builder-dabydat/SKILL.md` for gathering experience and writing specific achievements.

Ground claims in the user's actual work. Distinguish personal contributions from team results and production usage from prototypes or benchmarks. Include metrics where supported; never invent them.

Source repository: https://github.com/dabydat/resume-builder-skill
Installed from the `skill` directory on the `master` branch.
